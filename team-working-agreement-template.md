- Backlog Refinement cadence.

- Ticket update frequency.
- BAU capacity.
- PR review expectations.
- KT cadence.
- Communication channels.
- Decision process.
- Escalation path.
- Ticket closure và follow-up policy.
- Improvement cadence.
